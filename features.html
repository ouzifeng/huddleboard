Powerful Features for Remote Teams

Everything you need to collaborate effectively, visualize ideas, and work together seamlessly from anywhere in the world.

Real-Time Collaboration

Work together simultaneously with your team members. See cursors, edits, and changes in real-time. No more waiting for updates or dealing with version conflicts.

Digital Sticky Notes

Create, organize, and categorize ideas with colorful digital sticky notes. Drag and drop to reorganize, edit instantly, and never lose a brilliant idea again.

Mind Mapping & Flowcharts
