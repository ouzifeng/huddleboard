About HuddleBoard

We're on a mission to transform how remote teams collaborate and bring their ideas to life.

Our Mission

In a world where remote work has become the norm, we believe that distance shouldn't limit creativity or collaboration. HuddleBoard was born from the frustration of trying to brainstorm, plan, and visualize ideas across dispersed teams.

We're building the most intuitive, powerful, and inclusive digital whiteboarding platform that makes remote collaboration feel as natural and productive as working in the same room. Our goal is to break down barriers and unleash the creative potential of every team, everywhere.

Our Values

User-Centric

Every feature we build starts with understanding our users' needs. We prioritize simplicity, accessibility, and real value in everything we create.

Innovation

We're constantly pushing the boundaries of what's possible in digital collaboration, embracing new technologies and creative solutions.

Inclusivity

We believe great ideas come from diverse perspectives. Our platform is designed to be accessible to teams of all sizes, backgrounds, and abilities.

Our Story

HuddleBoard started in 2023 when our founders, frustrated by the limitations of existing collaboration tools, decided to build something better. Working remotely across three different time zones, they experienced firsthand the challenges of visual collaboration without physical whiteboards.

What began as a weekend project quickly grew into a vision for reimagining how distributed teams work together. We launched our beta with 50 early adopters and received overwhelming feedback about the platform's intuitive design and powerful collaboration features.

Today, HuddleBoard serves thousands of teams across 45+ countries, from small startups to Fortune 500 companies. We're backed by leading venture capital firms and continue to innovate at the intersection of design, technology, and human collaboration.

Our journey has just begun. We're excited to continue building tools that empower teams to create, collaborate, and bring their best ideas to life, no matter where they are in the world.
